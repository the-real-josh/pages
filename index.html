Sol Invictus

SRT

August 2025 - May 2026

  • Sol Invictus is a student-designed hybrid rocket designed to compete in the 30K hybrids category for the 2026 IREC (International Rocket Engineering Competition).
  • I designed the payload structure
    • I invented a novel snap-fit shelf-spine design that reduces the assembly time and difficulty.
    • I applied knowledge of mechanical design and engineering from all my different engineering experiences.
    • Communicated with payload electronics people to make sure their needs were met.
  • I designed the nosecone
    • Chose the geometry to provide optimal aerodynamics as well as maximum versatility and ease of integration for the payload RADAR team.
    • Created a bill of materials to purchase so that the nosecone could be manufactured.
    • Ran Ansys Explicit Dynamics and Ansys Static Structural simulations to model ground impact.
  • Designed the fincan
    • Applied knowledge of how minimum diameter sounding rocket fincans are designed.
    • Modeled the composite fincan in Ansys ACP Pre
    • Simulated the deflection of the fins and fincan under load to verify adequate strength.
    • Fincan design was ultimately not chosen for organization's internal reasons.
